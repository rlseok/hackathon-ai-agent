# Fold / lap / cold shut (forging)
- Short cracks where the metal surface folds back on itself, often at corners and at right angles to the surface.
- Causes: sharp die corners, improper die design, excessive chilling of the metal. Action [general practice]: treat as critical — isolate, check depth by penetrant/non-destructive test, fix die radii and material placement.

# Oxidation, scale and scale pits
- Heating a billet in a poor furnace atmosphere or for too long builds a scale layer; scale squeezed into the surface causes scale pits; scale left in dies causes pitting.
- Remedies: protective or inert furnace atmosphere, shorter exposure, clean the stock and dies, remove scale before final passes or machining. Decarburised areas and surface cracks are removed by grinding.
- Dark spots / stains are often linked to oxidation, contamination or heat tint (not confirmed by the sources — treat as a hypothesis to be verified by cleaning and re-inspection).

# Porosity (forged parts)
- Internal voids from inadequate pressure, poor ingots/billets or gas entrapment. Remedies: vacuum-degassed or ESR ingots, sufficient compressive force, tight temperature and time control.

# Scratches and surface damage
- Come from handling and tooling rather than forging itself: damaged billets, poor handling, die contamination, die wear. Remedies: inspect and clean billets, clean dies regularly, padded supports, protective film in transport, polishing/surface treatment for premium parts.

# Burrs and sharp edges
- Rough edges from shearing or laser cutting; cause poor fit and injury risk. Remedies: sharp maintained tools, deburring/grinding, waterjet or automated deburring.

# Corrosion
- Surface degradation from moisture, chemicals or harsh environments. Remedies: rust-resistant materials, coatings (paint, powder coat, galvanising), regular cleaning and inspection.
