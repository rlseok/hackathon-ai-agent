# 8D steps
- D0 Preparation and emergency response: plan the effort; record symptoms and emergency actions; decide whether a full 8D is needed.
- D1 Use a team with product/process knowledge.
- D2 Describe the problem quantitatively with 5W2H (who, what, where, when, why, how, how many); an Is/Is-Not worksheet helps.
- D3 Interim containment: isolate the problem from the customer (quarantine suspect lots, sort, 100% inspection).
- D4 Root cause and escape point: identify and verify all causes and why the defect was not caught; use 5 Whys and Ishikawa (fishbone, 4M).
- D5 Verify the permanent correction resolves the problem before release.
- D6 Implement and validate corrective actions, including the escape point.
- D7 Prevent recurrence: change management/operating systems, practices and procedures; apply to similar products.
- D8 Recognise the team.

# How it maps to this agent's reports
- NCR (reject): sections 1/4/6/7 feed D2, D3, D4 and D5–D7; an 8D is recommended for critical, multi-type or surface-wide defects.
- Reinspection request (review/Hold): do not release to the next process until the worker confirms; pass -> release and keep the image as a normal sample, fail -> issue an NCR.
