# Defect types inspected on assembled phones
- Scratches, cracks, chips, dents, misalignment and discoloration on housing and cover glass are checked after assembly and before packaging; manual inspection is inconsistent at production volume, so vision inspection is used.
- Multi-directional dark-field imaging helps reveal scratches across a whole cover glass.

# Stains versus real defects
- Stains (fingerprints, adhesive residue, cleaning-agent residue, dust) are frequently misjudged as defects and cause good products to be rejected. Hyperspectral work reached about 95% accuracy separating them.
- Practice [general, not from the sources]: re-clean the surface and inspect again before rejecting a suspected stain or oil contamination.

# Causes and prevention (factory view) — general practice, the cited pages do not analyse root causes
- Scratches: contact with hard/sharp objects, handling fixtures, trays and conveyors; use soft fixtures, protective film and cleaner handling.
- Oil / fingerprint contamination: handling without gloves, release agents and lubricants, incomplete cleaning; control gloves, cleaning steps and cleaning agent residue.
- Dents/chips on housing edges: drops and impacts in packing or transport; improve trays and dunnage.
- (Consumer guidance: keep phones away from keys/jewellery and use a case — not a factory control.)
